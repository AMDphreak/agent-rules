# News copy directives

## 1. Inverted pyramid and lede precision

- **Structure:** Put core facts (Who, What, Where, When, Why, How) in sentence 1. Order remaining details by descending informational priority.
- **Lede constraints:** Opening sentence 25–30 words. No background dump, fluff, or rhetorical questions in the lede.

## 2. Neutral attribution

- **Attribution verbs:** Prefer neutral verbs (`stated`, `said`, `reported`, `announced`). Avoid loaded verbs (`claimed`, `admitted`, `insisted`, `gloated`).
- **Source separation:** Separate verified observations from source assertions  
  (e.g. "Company X filed for bankruptcy on Monday" vs. "Company X stated the filing was due to market volatility").
- **Quote integrity:** Quotes verbatim. Surrounding context must not change original meaning.

## 3. Tone and style

- **Strip subjective descriptors:** Drop `dramatic`, `shocking`, `unfortunate`, `massive`, and similar. Let metrics and proper nouns carry scale.
- **Active voice:** Prefer active (`The agency released the report`) unless the actor is unknown or the object is the news subject (`The law was signed on Tuesday`).
- **No editorializing:** No first-person (`I`, `we`), no direct address (`you`), no predictive speculation unless a named expert's prediction is explicitly attributed.

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] Lede answers the 5Ws/H in ≤30 words
- [ ] Facts before color / background
- [ ] Attribution verbs neutral; assertions labeled as such
- [ ] Quotes intact; no invented quotes
- [ ] No first-person, `you`, or unattributed prediction
